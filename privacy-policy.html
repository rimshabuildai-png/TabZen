Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Overview

TabZen is an ADHD-friendly Chrome extension for tab management. This Privacy Policy explains what data TabZen accesses, how it is used, and what we never do with it. We built TabZen to protect your mental space — and that includes your data.

Data We Access

  • Tabs API: To read the titles, URLs, and favicons of your currently open tabs so they can be displayed and categorised inside the extension popup.
  • Storage API (chrome.storage.local): To save your sessions (Safe Spaces) and sprint timer state locally on your device only.

What We Do With Your Data

  • All data is stored only on your device using Chrome's built-in local storage.
  • Tab information is used only to display and organise tabs within the extension popup.
  • Saved sessions are stored only on your device and are never transmitted anywhere.

What We Never Do

  • Collect, transmit, or share your browsing history.
  • Send any data to external servers.
  • Sell, share, or monetise your data in any way.
  • Track your activity across websites.
  • Use analytics that collect personal information.

Permissions Explained

  • "tabs" permission: Required to read your currently open tabs. This data never leaves your device.
  • "storage" permission: Required to save your sessions between browser sessions. Stored locally only.
  • "host_permissions" (all URLs): Required only to load favicon icons for each tab. No page content is ever read or stored.

Children's Privacy

TabZen is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
